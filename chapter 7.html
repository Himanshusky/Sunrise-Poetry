Call
Chapter 07

After reaching home, the first thing he did was call her,
“Reached,” he said, and both felt relieved forever.

But due to network issues, the call got disconnected,
She called him again and said, “Out of all, ours was defected.”

Sadly, he said, “On our first call, there was a network defect,”
She smiled calmly and replied, “Some things are imperfectly perfect.”

“Imperfectly perfect… that’s what you said,”
“You know? Something like that already happened.”

“Our first introduction,” she completed his words,
“I asked the name of an idiot, and he froze at that moment—so weird.”

“First thing, who asks like that?”
“Who replies like that, you idiot?”

“Who randomly asks, ‘What’s your name?’”
“And who replies with, ‘Don’t you know my name?’”

Then she said, “I was just checking if you remembered your name ”
Then I replied, “Mine is Nischay… what about you, lame?”

“Someone who is never gonna leave,
My name is Naseeb.”

“That was the cheesiest line I’ve ever heard,”
Both laughed so hard while remembering that moment.

— Sunrise Poetry
